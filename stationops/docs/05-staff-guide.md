# 5. Staff guide — opening your station

*Print this and post it at the station.*

## Every opening

1. **Sign in.** Open StationOps on your phone or the station tablet and type your username and password.
2. **Tap "Start checklist".** If someone already started it, the button says **Continue checklist**, and you pick up where they left off.
3. **Do each task, then tap its box.** A green check means it's saved. Tap again if you tapped by mistake.
4. **Something wrong?** Tap **Report a problem** on that task and say what's wrong — for example, "Walk-in reads 45°F". Your manager sees it right away.
5. **Want to add a detail?** Tap **Add note**.
6. **When you're finished, tap "Review and submit".** Read the summary, then tap **Submit**.
7. You'll see **"Checklist submitted. Thank you!"** You're done.

## Good to know

- **Your progress saves every time you tap.** You can leave and come back.
- **Can't do a task?** Report a problem — don't tick it. An honest exception is better than a false check.
- **If it says "Could not save"**, check your Wi-Fi and tap again.
- **After you submit, the checklist is locked.** Tell your manager if something changes.
- **Forgot your password?** Ask a manager to reset it.
- **Signing out:** tap **Sign out** at the top, especially on a shared tablet.
