# 6. Manager training guide

About 20 minutes. Sign in with your manager account. Every manager page has the menu across the top.

## Daily routine (5 minutes, same time every morning)

1. **Dashboard.** The top row shows today's checklists: *Completed*, *In progress*, *With exceptions*, *Not started*, and how many *Need attention* over the last 30 days.
2. **Open issues.** Click any issue to see the full checklist. Deal with the problem, then type what was done in **Resolution** and click **Mark resolved**. Resolutions are permanent records, so be specific: who, what, when.
3. **Needs attention.** This lists checklists with open issues, today's exceptions, and earlier checklists that were started but never submitted. Follow up with the person named.

## Reading statuses

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Complete** (green) | Submitted, and every required task was done |
| **Submitted with exceptions** (red) | Submitted while a required task was not done or a problem was flagged |
| **In progress** (amber) | Started, not yet submitted |
| **Not started** | No checklist for that station today |

The required-done figure (e.g. 6/7) counts **required** tasks only.

## Changing the checklist

**Checklists → Edit tasks.** Add, reword, reorder, remove, or switch Required on/off, then **Save new version**. Past checklists are never changed. Note what you changed and why in **What changed?**. See doc 3 for how to write good tasks.

## History and exports

**History** filters by dates, employee, station, status, or a word from notes (try "cooler"). Click a date to open that checklist.

- **Export CSV (summary)** gives one row per checklist.
- **Export CSV (every task)** gives one row per task, with notes, times and resolutions.

Times are in the restaurant's time zone (named in the column header).

## Staff

**Staff → Add a person.** Give them a name, username, role, station and a temporary password, and share it privately. To **reset a password**, type a new one in that person's row and click **Save**; they are signed out everywhere. When someone leaves, set **Access → Turned off**; their past records stay. Accounts lock for 15 minutes after 5 wrong passwords, and resetting the password unlocks them.

## Pilot metric

**Pilot metric → Add a period.**

- **Baseline:** the weeks before StationOps. Enter the expected count from the schedule and the number of fully completed paper checklists, choosing "Entered manually".
- **Pilot:** the StationOps weeks. Enter the expected count from the schedule; completions are counted from StationOps records.

Only checklists submitted with every required task done count as completed. Missing, unsubmitted and exception checklists count against the rate. The comparison is two numbers, not proof of cause — note anything unusual (short-staffed days, holidays) in the period notes.
