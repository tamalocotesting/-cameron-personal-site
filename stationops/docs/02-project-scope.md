# 2. Project scope (one page)

**Project:** StationOps pilot — digital station opening checklist
**Location:** ______________________ **Station(s):** ______________________
**Pilot dates:** ____________ to ____________ (two weeks)

## Problem

Station openings are checked on paper or not at all. Steps get missed, problems reach managers late or not at all, and there is no reliable record of who did what.

## Goal

Every opening of the pilot station is checked in StationOps. Problems reach the manager the moment they are flagged, and the restaurant can measure checklist completion against a baseline.

## In scope

- One station, one opening checklist (manager-approved tasks)
- Employee phone workflow: done, notes, flag a problem, submit
- Manager dashboard, issue resolution, history, CSV export
- Manager-maintained checklist editor (versioned) and staff accounts
- Completion-rate measurement for a baseline period and the pilot period
- Training for employees and managers; acceptance testing; go-live sign-off

## Out of scope

Billing, payments, multiple locations, scheduling, POS or inventory integration, payroll, AI features, native apps, closing or mid-shift checklists, offline mode.

## Success measure

Checklist completion rate for the pilot period, compared with the baseline if one exists. No target is promised in advance; the result is reported as measured.

## Responsibilities

| Who | Responsible for |
| --- | --- |
| Restaurant manager | Approving checklist wording, the expected-count source, training staff on shift, resolving flagged issues, final sign-off |
| Implementation lead | Configuration, accounts, training materials, UAT, issue tracking, measurement report, handoff |
| Employees | Completing the checklist each opening; flagging problems |

## Risks

| Risk | Mitigation |
| --- | --- |
| Staff skip the app on busy mornings | Keep the checklist short; manager checks the dashboard at a fixed time each morning |
| Poor Wi-Fi at the station | Test during UAT; failed saves show an error and can be retried |
| Shared logins | Each person gets their own account; managers turn off accounts when people leave |
| No reliable baseline | Report pilot numbers alone and say so |
